# CONTEXT — Geometry Dash Remake (SDL3 + C++)

## Glossary
- **Remake**: Inspired-by clone with original assets; no ripped sprites/music.
- **Level**: Data-defined obstacle course; 10 hand-designed levels with rising difficulty, designed by a Claude sub-agent.
- **Gamemode**: Cube (jump) and Ship (hold-to-fly) modes.
- **Portal**: Gravity and speed portals.
- **Practice mode**: Checkpoint-based low-stakes mode.

## Decisions
- Cube + ship, gravity/speed portals, spikes/blocks/platforms, practice mode, attempts counter, one song per level, instant death-restart.
- 10 original levels; art/music sourced by sub-agents (open-license).
